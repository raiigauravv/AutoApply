The Workflow

Four steps.
Zero spreadsheets.

01

Configure your profile

Set your target roles, locations, work authorization, salary target, and scoring weights. Paste your master CV. Takes 2 minutes. The AI learns from your feedback over time.

02

Set the scanner

Run the portal scanner to collect jobs from 45+ companies. AutoApply grades each one against your CV and returns a ranked list. Set a schedule and it runs while you sleep.

03

Apply on autopilot

For roles scoring B+ or higher: generate a tailored PDF resume, fill the ATS form, write cold outreach, and prep STAR stories — all in one window. No copy-pasting between tabs.

04

Track & adapt

The tracker follows every role from applied → interview → offer. The pattern analyzer identifies weak spots in your funnel and adjusts your personal scoring model automatically.
